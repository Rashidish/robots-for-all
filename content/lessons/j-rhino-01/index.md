## Predict

Rhinos need water every day. Show the mission **Rhino to the water** on the screen.

Ask the class:

- Where is the rhino? Where is the water? Where is the rock?
- If the rhino goes forward once and then turns right, will it bump the rock?

## Run

1. Tape a four-by-four grid on the floor and copy the board: the rhino in the top left corner facing right, the water in the bottom right corner, the rock in the second row.
2. Children plan the arrow cards together and lay them in a row.
3. A child is the rhino and walks the plan. Did the rhino reach the water without bumping the rock?
4. On the screen, tap the same blocks into the row and press **Play**.

## Try

1. Try a plan that bumps the rock on purpose. The rhino stops and says "Oops, a rock!". Which arrow made it bump?
2. A wrong arrow in a plan is called a **bug**. Fix the bug and play again.
3. Find a different way to the water. Is it longer or shorter?
4. Children who are ready can try the **×3** block: it does the next block three times.

## Make

In pairs on the floor grid, move the rock to a new square. One child plans a way to the water, the other checks the plan by walking it. Then swap.

Colour the stars and a face on the mission sheet.

## Teacher notes

- The big idea is **debugging**: finding the arrow that went wrong. Ask "which card was the bug?" rather than "what did you do wrong?".
- Children who count the squares and turns before playing usually succeed first time; point this out.
- The ×3 block is the topic of the next lesson. It's fine if only a few children try it today.
