## Predict

Show the mission **Bus to school** on the screen. Talk about the way to school: where does the bus have to turn?

Ask the class:

- How many squares does the bus go up before it can turn?
- Which repeat card would help with the long straight part?

## Run

1. In groups, children lay out a plan with arrow cards and repeat cards. Each group shows its plan to the class.
2. Tap one group's plan into the row and press **Play**.
3. If the bus bumps a rock, play again and watch closely: which block was lit when it bumped? Fix that part of the plan.

## Try

1. Count the blocks in each group's plan. Which plan is shortest?
2. Can you reach the school with eight blocks? That's the best way, with two stars.
3. Try the plan on a five-by-five floor grid with paper rocks, with one child as the bus.

## Make

In groups, design a route to school on the floor grid: put the rocks and the school where you like. Another group plans the route with the fewest cards they can and walks it to test it.

Colour the stars and a face on the mission sheet.

## Teacher notes

- This mission brings together forward, turns and repeats. Expect several tries: testing and fixing is the lesson.
- Encourage groups to test long plans in parts: "play just the first three blocks, then add more".
- Road safety links well here: real buses also follow a route and stop for obstacles.
- Children who finish all six Junior lessons are ready for Phase 1 in Grade 3, starting with the rolling cardboard cart.
