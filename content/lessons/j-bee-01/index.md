## Predict

Show the mission **Bee to the flower** on the screen, but don't press anything yet.

Ask the class:

- How many squares is the bee from the flower? Let's count together.
- How many **forward** blocks will the bee need?

## Run

1. A child holds up the forward cards they think the bee needs, before anyone taps.
2. Tap the same blocks into the row, one for each card.
3. Press **Play**. Did the bee land on the flower?
4. If the bee stopped too soon or bumped the edge, count again and fix the row.

## Try

1. Make a row of squares on the floor with tape and put a flower at the end. Children take turns to be the bee.
2. Move the flower one square further away. How many forward cards now?
3. Back on the screen: can you reach the flower with the fewest blocks? Two stars means the best way!

## Make

In pairs, one child places the flower anywhere along the row of squares. The other child lays out the arrow cards to reach it, then walks the plan to test it. Then swap.

Colour the stars and a face on the mission sheet.

## Teacher notes

- The big idea is **plan, then test**: children put the cards down first, and only then the robot moves. Resist the urge to let them tap one block at a time and watch.
- A wrong plan is not a mistake, it's a test. Praise children who find and fix their own plan.
- Children who finish early can try the mission again and count the blocks in the row out loud.
